Panel transitions

Eight ways for one method panel to hand off to the next. Same four panels, same palette and type in every one, so the only thing that differs is the move. Pick one and I'll build it into the section.

The last two are the newest — both keep the whole set on screen at once, so the four points read as one argument rather than four slides.

Each page is standalone — open it directly, no server needed. scroll or drag the bar to scrub, ← → to step one panel, space to autoplay. Park mid-transition to inspect the geometry; the readout shows which hand-off and how far through it you are.

  1. 01 Cube rollFour panels are four faces of one cube. The scroll rolls it, so the next panel comes up from below on a hard edge and the whole screen is a single rigid object. Depth · whole-screenReads as · mechanismCost · low
  2. 02 Hinge doorsThe panel splits down the middle and both halves swing open toward you on edge hinges, catching light on their inner faces. The next panel is held one screen back and comes forward through the gap. Depth · toward cameraReads as · revealCost · low
  3. 03 Slat flipThe screen is nine vertical blades. Each turns edge-on and comes back carrying the next panel, staggered left to right, so the change crosses the screen as a wave rather than arriving all at once. Depth · per-bladeReads as · waveCost · medium
  4. 04 Portal diveThe next panel is a lit window far out in the dark. The camera flies through the current one — which scales past and out of frame — and lands on it as it opens to full bleed. Star streaks stretch with the speed. Depth · camera dollyReads as · falling inCost · medium
  5. 05 Deck throwThe panels are a physical stack of slabs receding into depth. The top one tips, lifts and is thrown past the camera; the deck steps forward. The only option with visible depth while parked, not just mid-move. Depth · at rest tooReads as · objectsCost · low
  6. 06 Tile shatterA four-by-three grid. Outgoing tiles tumble toward the camera along their own radius from the centre while the incoming ones arrive out of depth on the opposite tilt. The most maximal of the six. Depth · per-tileReads as · burstCost · high
  7. 07 Stack buildThe Lenis move. Each card is dealt in from the lower right, oversized and tilted, and lands on a stack that never clears — every point already made stays on screen, receding toward a vanishing point up-left and ghosting through the card in front. Depth · at rest tooReads as · an argumentCost · low
  8. 08 Corridor diveThe portal dive made continuous. All four panels hang at their own depth down one corridor and the camera flies through them, so the ones you have not reached are already visible ahead through the one you are reading. Star streaks stretch with the speed. Depth · camera dollyReads as · one shaftCost · medium
